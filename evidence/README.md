# Ishihara native evidence

The PNGs show the face at native resolution on the named Pebble emulator.
Each platform report ties screenshots and logs to the installed PBW's SHA-256.
The validation summary lists the six targets.

The standard views cover 10:08, the next minute, 12:00, 03:15, and 18:30.
Rollover views show the approach to noon and midnight, followed by the new
hour. Additional captures show the hands off, on, and still on after relaunch.

Inspection covers smooth numeral shapes, irregular dot packing, visible gaps,
color palette contrast, monochrome filled and outlined circles, the small time
caption, and both hand states. The plate stays still between minute ticks.

These captures establish emulator behavior. Battery life, wrist-tap behavior,
and daylight readability on physical watches remain unmeasured.
